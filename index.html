Scaling a Fintech Product to 7 Million Users

During my tenure at bKash, I owned the Savings Product, transforming it from a stagnant feature to a massive growth engine.

Phase 1: Diagnosis

The Stagnant Baseline

When taking over the savings engine, total active subscriptions were stuck at 0.7M, with a bare-minimum growth rate of only 20k-30k monthly subscriptions. The product suffered from friction-filled onboarding and complex workflows.

Assistant Product Manager, Financial Products
Phase 2: Distribution Scale

Scaling User Acquisition

Leveraged both digital & physical networks to capture marginalized target markets. I initiated a Weekly DPS (starting at BDT 250/$2) for daily wage earners, integrated $150 Monthly DPS for professionals, and activated bKash's rural agent network alongside localized success-story campaigns.

Key Growth Result

Subscriptions soared to 7M+. Scaled monthly intake by 10x (200k–250k new users every month).

Phase 3: Optimization

Revamping the UX Loop

Identified deep friction points in user flows, which had a poor baseline funnel conversion rate of 1.5%. I guided screen-by-screen UX research to compress the convoluted 10-tap registration model into a seamless 3-tap onboarding checkout.

Conversion Metric

Funnel activation leaped from 1.5% directly to almost 35% conversion.

Phase 4: Operations

Proactive Churn Mitigation

Traditional operations took months to resolve user disputes, resulting in high abandonment rates. I introduced real-time monitoring structures and automated daily SQL sanity routines to spot anomalies. This enabled us to tackle issues within 24-48 hours.

Retention Impact

Account maturity rates zoomed upwards from a mere 3.7% up to a stellar 26% overall retention.

7M

Total Combined Portfolio Impact

Referencing our documentation in "case studies at bKash.docx", these optimizations collectively brought bKash Savings to more than 7 Million total subscriptions within a 3.5-year horizon.
